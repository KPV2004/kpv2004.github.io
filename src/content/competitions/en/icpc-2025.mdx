## Event Overview

The **International Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC)** is the premier global competitive programming competition for university students.

## Key Highlights

- **National Level**: Represented Kasetsart University Sriracha Campus at the national finals.
- **Problem Solving under Pressure**: Solved complex mathematical and algorithmic challenges within strict 5-hour time limits.
- **Core Skills**: Graph Theory, Dynamic Programming, Segment Trees, and C++ Optimization.

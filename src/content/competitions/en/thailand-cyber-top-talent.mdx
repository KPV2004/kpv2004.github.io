## Event Overview

**Thailand Cyber Top Talent** is a major national Capture The Flag (CTF) competition organized by NCSA Thailand to cultivate cybersecurity expertise.

![Thailand Cyber Top Talent 2023 Certificate](../../../assets/images/ThailandCyberTopTalent2023.png)

## Key Technical Skills Demonstrated

- **Web Exploitation & Reverse Engineering**: Analyzed binary security vulnerabilities and web application exploits.
- **Forensics & Cryptography**: Decoded encrypted ciphers and performed digital memory forensics.
